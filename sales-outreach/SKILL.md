# Draft outreach

Resolve the owner through `business`. Read the qualified account brief, relevant evidence and the owner's `voice.md`; without a brief, use `sales-qualify` first. If a voice profile is needed, follow `business/references/sales.md` and use an export of the owner's sent mail. Review the resulting profile with the owner.

Draft a specific, honest message with a clear next step. Check capacity before offering a date and obtain the founder's decision on any price or terms. Record the conversation and dated next step with `kaito-leads.mjs touch`. Send the exact draft to `sales-review`. Sending is exclusively through `email-send` and its exact-payload approval gate; this skill never sends.
