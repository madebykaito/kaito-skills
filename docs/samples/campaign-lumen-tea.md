# Lumen Tea Co. first-flush campaign

Fictional working example for owner `lumen-tea`. This is a draft campaign, not an approved or published campaign. Product details, dates, prices, stock and results need owner confirmation.

## Brief

- Goal: introduce the first-flush Darjeeling tea and invite interested people to join a launch list. Measure visits and sign-ups with tagged links once tracking is ready.
- Audience: people who enjoy loose-leaf tea and want to learn about seasonal Darjeeling harvests.
- Message: meet the first flush, with a quiet moment to brew and taste it.
- Channels: Instagram organic posts, a short paid social test and email to people who opted in. Use the owner's confirmed account and consent records before sending.
- Offer: join the launch list for availability updates. No price, stock promise or deadline is assumed.
- Evidence needed: confirm the tea's origin, harvest, tasting notes and availability before making specific claims. Do not imply a health benefit.
- Budget and schedule: owner to set before booking ads or scheduling posts. Baseline and targets: owner to agree before launch.
