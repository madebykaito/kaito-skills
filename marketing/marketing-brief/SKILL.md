# Marketing brief

Resolve the owner with `node <store>/business/scripts/kaito-owner.mjs resolve --owner <id>`. Read only that owner's brand, voice, audience, offers and evidence. Ask for missing buyer language and proof; do not invent either. Check whether the offer and evidence can support the proposed piece.

Write a brief with one buyer, their problem and existing belief, one message, permitted proof and its sources, one action, a measurable target and date, and what the piece excludes. Ask the founder to agree before drafting. A change in buyer, message or proof needs a revised brief.

For the mechanical review and retro, save a JSON brief with string fields `buyer`, `message`, `action`, `metric` and a numeric `target`. Keep the full brief alongside it. Match message and action to the intended wording in the piece.
