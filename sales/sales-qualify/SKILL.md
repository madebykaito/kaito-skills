# Qualify a lead

Start with `business`: resolve one owner and read only that owner's relevant brand, offers, evidence and lead history. Import the lead with `kaito-leads.mjs import`, or list existing leads as described in `business/references/sales.md`.

Record the buyer's situation and source, budget, decision authority, need and timing. Mark each fact known, assumed or unknown; assumptions become questions. Check offer fit and actual delivery capacity. Give the founder a go, no-go or the one question that decides it. Once agreed, record the brief and next step with `move` and `touch`, including a callback date when appropriate. Do not contact the lead. Pass a qualified brief to `sales-outreach`.
