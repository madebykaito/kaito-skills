# Review sales material

Resolve the owner through `business`. Read the exact draft and the qualified brief. Stop if either is missing. Compare every price, term and date with approved source material, check delivery capacity, trace claims to owner-scoped evidence, and remove private costs or another client's information. Check scope and the next step.

Take the buyer's perspective: identify reasons to doubt, delay or reject, including price. Separate evidence from speculation and do not invent answers. Return PASS or FAIL with specific findings and their sources. Fail on unsupported claims, inconsistent terms, missing next steps or unresolved serious risks. Fix and review again before the founder approves the exact payload through `email-send`. Record the review with `kaito-leads.mjs move`; never send from this skill.
