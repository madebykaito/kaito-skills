# Sales retro

Resolve the owner through `business`. Read the account brief, touches, review and the founder's account of the outcome. Record won, lost or stalled and the buyer's stated reason; if none was given, say so instead of guessing. Compare the outcome with qualification assumptions and review objections. Recommend what to repeat, stop or change in qualification, offer and follow-up.

Record the outcome with `kaito-leads.mjs move --stage retro --note ...` and close completed callback queue items through `kaito-ledger.mjs queue close`. Add useful buyer language or confirmed evidence to owner-scoped memory only with appropriate permission. Do not put deal values or margins in memory, or publish a buyer's identity or words without permission.
